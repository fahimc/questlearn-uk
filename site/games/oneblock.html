    Getting started

    Turn one block into an island.

    1. Look down and Mine the glowing centre block. It returns straight away.
    2. Choose an owned block. Look down at the top or side of an island block and press Place—the nearest edge extends automatically.
    3. Hold Shift on desktop or switch on Sneak on touch to stop at ledges while you build.
    4. Reach 10 mined blocks and solve the question gate. Learn and Hint are always optional.
    5. If you jump or step into the void, you return to the centre without losing your work.
