# Game plan 2: Skybound Academy

## Promise

Climb a spectacular shifting tower where clear thinking reveals routes. The game borrows the expressive movement and rapid retries of obstacle-course platformers while removing public ranking, humiliation and monetised skips.

## Core loop

`Preview route → move/jump → reach calm platform → solve or apply → route changes → retry/advance → short recap`.

Learning prompts only occur on safe platforms or before a route choice; they never interrupt live movement. A correct answer may reveal the most direct route. An incorrect answer triggers feedback and a slightly scaffolded route—not loss of all progress.

## Game feel

- responsive acceleration, coyote time, jump buffering and strong landing feedback;
- 3–6 minute tower segments with checkpoints;
- authored obstacle grammar plus seeded combinations (not unsafe procedural randomness);
- camera comfort, motion reduction and generous collision options;
- personal bests and route mastery, never public rank by child.

## Learning fit

Best for short maths retrieval, vocabulary/morphology, grammar, science classification, sequencing/algorithms, map directions and chronology. Some challenges are physical representations: jump to factor-pair platforms; order fractions by height; follow an algorithmic route; rotate by an angle; land on evidence that supports a claim.

## Difficulty has two axes

Motor challenge and learning challenge adapt independently. A learner struggling with jumps should not receive easier mathematics by default, and vice versa. Teacher/learner controls can lock motor assistance: wider platforms, slower hazards, untimed route and reduced camera movement.

## Session and wellbeing

Five stages form one complete climb. The end offers a recap and equal-weight “finish for now”/“choose another tower” actions. No streak-loss, infinite climb or notification pressure.

## MVP

One themed tower, keyboard/touch controls, five checkpoint challenges, separate motor assist, local progress and maths content. Prototype: [`site/games/skybound.html`](../../site/games/skybound.html).

## Risks and tests

- **Physical skill masks learning:** compare objective evidence at different motor-assist settings.
- **Prompt breaks flow:** observe children and measure abandon rates at placement points.
- **Speed becomes anxiety:** untimed default for prompts; never score reading speed unless explicitly assessing fluency.
- **Motion sensitivity:** reduced camera effects, FOV control and 2D fallback.
