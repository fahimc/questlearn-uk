# Blocksmith Worlds: 20 maths build questions

These child-facing questions follow the England Years 3–5 progression documented in [`docs/research/england-years-3-5.md`](../research/england-years-3-5.md). They state the available total and the mathematical rule, but never state the derived material counts, row length, tower height or missing cuboid dimension. The learner's placed blocks are the answer.

## Feedback policy

Before building, the interface may show a calculation strategy but not a calculated answer. After a check, feedback may confirm the total is high or low, identify a material/shape mismatch and repeat the strategy. The numerical solution is only stated after a correct build.

The internal validator checks total blocks, material mix and—where required—rectangle, outline, cuboid or contiguous tower geometry. These internal solution values are implementation data and must not be rendered in the quest dialog or journal.
