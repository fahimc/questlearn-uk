# Year 4 deep question-bank review

## Learner and bank

Year 4 normally serves ages 8–9 in England. The runtime provides **300 Year 4 questions**: 100 each for English, Maths and Science. Five 20-question levels create an in-year ramp from recognition to application.

| Level | English focus | Maths focus | Science focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | expanded noun phrases, pronouns and spelling | 4-digit place value and rounding | classification and habitats |
| 2 | fronted adverbials, tense and vocabulary | multiplication, division and factor pairs | digestion, teeth and food chains |
| 3 | paragraphs, speech and inference | fractions, decimals and money | states of matter and changes of state |
| 4 | apostrophes, cohesion and summary | perimeter, area, angles and coordinates | sound, pitch, volume and vibration |
| 5 | integrated grammar, reading and editing | conversion, charts and multi-step problems | circuits, conductors, fair tests and evidence |

The bank prioritises the Year 4 multiplication expectation, secure written/mental number relationships and first-quadrant spatial reasoning. English prompts put punctuation and grammar inside short, complete sentences. Science items distinguish processes such as evaporation from properties such as conductivity and require evidence-based choices where suitable.

## Game review

- Each wall can be answered independently in a short play pause.
- Distractors model likely misconceptions but avoid tiny wording traps.
- Number values and reading demands fit the selected year.
- Learn panels teach the live concept, not a generic subject summary.
- Set partitioning provides four 25-wall worlds before a source question repeats.

Runtime files: `site/games/question-banks/year-4/english.js`, `maths.js` and `science.js`.

Primary alignment: Department for Education programmes of study for [English](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study), [mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study) and [science](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).
