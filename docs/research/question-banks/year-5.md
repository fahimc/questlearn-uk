# Year 5 deep question-bank review

## Learner and bank

Year 5 normally serves ages 9–10 in England. The runtime provides **300 Year 5 questions**: 100 each for English, Maths and Science. The five levels remain within Year 5 while moving from accurate recall to connected reasoning.

| Level | English focus | Maths focus | Science focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | relative clauses, vocabulary and spelling | large-number place value and rounding | life cycles and reproduction |
| 2 | modal verbs, parenthesis and cohesion | factors, multiples, primes and calculation | human development and material properties |
| 3 | inference, summary and author choices | equivalent, mixed and multiplied fractions | solutions, mixtures and reversible changes |
| 4 | punctuation, register and editing | decimals, percentages and negative numbers | Earth, Moon, Sun and planetary movement |
| 5 | integrated comprehension and composition choices | area, volume, conversion and multi-step problems | gravity, air resistance, mechanisms and evidence |

Maths questions preserve the relationships children must reason about rather than rewarding random guessing. English tasks use compact passages or sentences with all required evidence visible. Science wording distinguishes models from observations and keeps claims at primary-school precision.

## Game review

- More demanding questions still resolve to one clear wall action.
- No item depends on unseen diagrams, long passages or teacher-only context.
- Spelling clues teach useful morphology or letter patterns without showing the answer.
- Learn content uses alternate examples so support does not become an answer reveal.
- IDs, prompts and source batches are deterministic for review and bug reproduction.

Runtime files: `site/games/question-banks/year-5/english.js`, `maths.js` and `science.js`.

Primary alignment: Department for Education programmes of study for [English](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study), [mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study) and [science](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).
