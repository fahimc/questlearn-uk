# Year 3 deep question-bank review

## Learner and bank

Year 3 normally serves ages 7–8 in England. The runtime provides **300 Year 3 questions**: 100 each for English, Maths and Science. Each subject is split into five gameplay levels of 20, so challenge rises within Year 3 without leaking later-year objectives.

| Level | English focus | Maths focus | Science focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | word classes, sentence sense, common spellings | place value, sequences, compare and order | plant parts, nutrition, skeletons, rocks |
| 2 | conjunctions, prefixes and vocabulary | mental addition/subtraction, inverse checks | plant needs, muscles, fossils and soils |
| 3 | homophones, tense and sentence punctuation | 3, 4 and 8 times tables; division | light, shadows, magnets and forces |
| 4 | paragraphs, prepositions and direct speech | unit fractions, time and money | reflection, magnetic materials and friction |
| 5 | retrieval, inference and concise explanation | perimeter, shape turns, measures and problems | fair tests and evidence across Year 3 topics |

English spelling records draw from age-appropriate statutory or curriculum-linked patterns, with clues that do not contain the answer. Reading items keep the evidence in the prompt so a child does not need hidden context. Maths numbers remain inside Year 3 expectations and ask one visible operation or judgement at a time. Science questions separate observation, explanation and fair-test thinking.

## Game review

- Prompts are brief enough to read after reaching a wall.
- Four-choice questions have one unambiguous answer and three distinct distractors.
- Spelling walls contain exactly the letters required.
- Hints point to a strategy or feature rather than giving the answer.
- Learn support names the topic, models a method with a different example and asks the child to check their own choice.

Runtime files: `site/games/question-banks/year-3/english.js`, `maths.js` and `science.js`.

Primary alignment: Department for Education programmes of study for [English](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study), [mathematics](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study) and [science](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).
