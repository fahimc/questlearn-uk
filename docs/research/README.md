# Curriculum research index

Research reviewed 29 August 2026. Primary official sources were preferred; evidence summaries are clearly separated from product recommendations.

## What “UK curriculum” means

Education is devolved. England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have different curricula, age groupings and progression models. A product for “UK ages 7–10” must therefore store both the underlying skill and the nation-specific curriculum reference.

The detailed content baseline in this repository is England, where ages 7–10 normally fall across Years 3, 4 and 5 in Key Stage 2. The cross-UK model is in [devolved-nations.md](devolved-nations.md).

## Product conclusion

The strongest early product wedge is maths fluency plus applied science and reading comprehension. These have clear progression, short assessable interactions and natural links to building, movement and narrative. History, geography, computing and design technology should appear as integrated mission contexts rather than interchangeable quiz skins.

Every content item should carry: jurisdiction, stage/year, subject, objective ID, objective wording or licensed paraphrase, prerequisite skills, interaction type, misconception tags, feedback model, accessibility variants, evidence rule and review date.
